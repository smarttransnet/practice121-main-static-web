Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up Practice 121 for your clinic.

How does the Sinhala AI Medical Scribe work? +
Keep Practice 121 open on your smartphone or laptop while you consult. The ambient AI listens to the natural dialogue in Sinhala or English, converts clinical details into standardized SOAP notes, and generates prescription suggestions in seconds.
Do I need to buy special microphones or high-end laptops? +
No special equipment is needed. Practice 121 runs entirely through your existing web browser on any standard smartphone, tablet, or laptop. It does not burden your device storage or memory (RAM).
Can I edit and amend notes and prescriptions before sending? +
Yes, absolutely. The doctor has 100% control. You can review, modify dosages, add items from your favorite medication list, and approve every record before it is finalized or dispatched.
How do patients receive their prescriptions? +
Once you approve the prescription, an instant digital prescription link is dispatched directly to the patient's mobile phone via SMS or web link, which can be presented at any pharmacy.
Can walk-in patients join the queue without booking online? +
Yes. You can place a QR code at your clinic front door. Walk-in patients can scan it with their phone camera to register and join your live queue, or you can register them at the desk in seconds.
Is patient health data secure and compliant with Sri Lankan laws? +
Yes. Practice 121 is fully compliant with Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA No. 9 of 2022). All communications and records are protected using bank-grade AES-256 encryption.
